How it works

  • Press START when you begin work.
  • Press PAUSE during breaks; break time is tracked separately.
  • Press RESUME to continue your active time.
  • Reminders appear at 15, 10, 5 minutes and 10 seconds remaining.
  • At 8 hours: “Your 8-hour work target is complete! Please turn off MACH.”
